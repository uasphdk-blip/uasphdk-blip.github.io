Intended use

This utility is for personal operation of accounts and application data that the owner is authorized to manage. Grant access only from the intended Google account and use the configured private backup folder.

Recovery responsibilities

Keep the backup passphrase separately and verify restore evidence before relying on a backup. An encrypted file cannot be recovered without the required passphrase. A backup represents a point in time; later accepted application writes must be preserved or reconciled before replacing a production database.

Availability and free-service limits

Provider quotas, authorization expiry and service availability can delay or stop jobs. This personal-use setup provides no continuous-availability guarantee. Scheduled operation is not active merely because an OAuth client exists; activation requires separate successful execution evidence.

The configured plan permits free services only. Exhausting free quotas does not authorize adding billing, purchasing storage or enabling paid usage automatically.

Stopping use

The owner may disable scheduled jobs, revoke the application's access in Google Account settings, and manage its backup files in Google Drive. Revocation does not remove already stored files. Read the privacy policy for the data-handling description.

Support

Contact the support email displayed on the OAuth consent screen. Share safe error reports only; keep credentials and decrypted data private.
